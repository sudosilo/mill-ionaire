Mill-ionaire changelog

2026-11-06, v2026.11.06-2. Order, lathe, and brake programs now resume when the game is reopened without a crash. After a power drop on reopen, floor machines trip their emergency stops for the crew to reset, and your mill boots before its program resumes. The crew looks at any machine that sits still and says why in a bubble, and says what they fix while they work. Lasers can now run out of assist gas. Checks for the finance minute and the machine settings line are steadier.

2026-11-05, v2026.11.05. Added a warehouse with three times the floor, flatbed truck orders, and specialist crews for new breakdowns. Added an office building with sales managers who ship ready orders and linguistics engineers who spell spoken orders. Added laser cutters and metal printers, spare parts, and a statistics page in the menu. The caller's voice now plays even when shop sound is muted. Machines keep working when others stop, and every machine has a work the order queue button.

2026-11-04, v2026.11.04. Added spoken orders. A caller says the word, you spell it into its slots, and a wrong spelling docks half the price. Made the check for the hand bend steadier.

2026-10-30, v2026.10.30. Added a cash out button that sends your score to the shared high score board. You can add an optional link beside your tag, and it shows under the tag on the board. High scores now have a place in the menu.

2026-10-26, v2026.10.26. Redeployed the five axis CNC update.

2026-10-25, v2026.10.25. Added an engine lathe with its own tab that turns parts like studs, standoffs, and sign posts. Added a press brake that bends brackets, channels, box frames, and giant shapes for storefront sign orders. Inventory bins now show every finish with counts and open orders. Added a five axis CNC that cuts letters and turned parts faster with less wear, and a broke shop now keeps its own mill running on scrap stock.

2026-10-23, v2026.10.23. Added a how to play README. Number and emoji dies, matching sets, a spelling bee for long words, and a stamping press, powder coat booth, and LED bench on a finishing line. The game also starts faster.

2026-10-21, v2026.10.21. Mill-ionaire version two arrives as a full game. Customers post word orders, shipping them earns cash, and the shop sells new finishes and floor mills. Added wages and a power bill, a chip conveyor, coolant and software upgrades, shop sound, a flashing getting started marker, saved games, and a playtest check that runs before every build. Running costs were later cut to a quarter.

2026-10-11, v2026.10.11. First release. A knee mill simulator where you move the table, pick a cutter, and cut letters out of metal.
